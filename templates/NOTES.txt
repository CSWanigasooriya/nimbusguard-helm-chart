Thank you for installing NimbusGuard!

To check the status of your deployment:
  kubectl get all -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

To view operator logs:
  kubectl logs deployment/{{ include "nimbusguard.fullname" . }}-operator

If you enabled Prometheus, metrics are exposed at /metrics on the consumer workload and operator pods.

If you enabled Loki and Tempo, logs and traces are available in Grafana (if installed).

For more information, visit:
  https://github.com/your-org/nimbusguard
