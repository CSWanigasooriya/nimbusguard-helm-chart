🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING:
If KEDA shows connection errors:
1. Check Prometheus readiness: kubectl get pods -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} -l app.kubernetes.io/name=prometheus
2. Test Prometheus query: kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} svc/prometheus 9090:9090
3. Restart KEDA operator: kubectl rollout restart deployment keda-operator -n keda
